Built 2 companies in 3 years. No pitch decks, no consultants.

A 10-person venture studio in Los Angeles building AI companies from scratch. We originate ideas, ship products in 60 days, and spin out ventures that hit 10+ paying customers. In-house teams, shared infrastructure, evidence-based decisions.

The studio model

Traditional venture capital funds dozens of bets hoping a few pay off. We build fewer companies, but we build them right—from scratch, in-house, with a shared team.

We don't wait for founders to walk through the door. We spot opportunities in overlooked markets, talk to users, and shape ideas worth testing. Then we build fast—product, design, and growth working in one loop.

When a venture shows real traction, we spin it out as a standalone company. If it doesn't, we shut it down and carry the learnings forward. Evidence decides, not sunk cost.

How we work

Three phases. One team. No theater.

01

Originate (90 days)

We look for problems worth solving in markets others overlook. We conduct 20-30 user interviews, analyze competitive positioning, and shape ideas with real potential. Kill or commit by day 90.

  • Market research and user interviews
  • Problem validation and opportunity sizing
  • Competitive analysis and positioning
  • Business model hypotheses
02

Build (6 months)

Product, design, and growth work together from day one. Ship first version within 60 days. Iterate weekly based on real user data. Target first revenue by month 6.

  • Rapid prototyping and user testing
  • Product-market fit experiments
  • Go-to-market strategy and execution
  • Data-driven iteration cycles
03

Launch (12 months)

Ventures showing clear product-market fit spin out as standalone companies with dedicated teams and external capital. Target profitability by month 12. No traction? Shut down cleanly.

  • Spinout as independent entity
  • Dedicated leadership and team
  • External funding and governance
  • Ongoing studio support and learnings

What makes us different

We're not a consultancy that hands off a prototype. We're not an incubator that takes meetings and gives advice. We're builders who own the full loop—from first line of code to first paying customer to first 10,000 users.

Strategy, product, and go-to-market stay in one place. Learnings from one venture inform the next. No silos, no handoffs, no theater. Just the work.

When a venture proves itself, we package the tech for other companies—battle-tested infrastructure you can deploy in weeks.

Full-stack team

Product, design, engineering, and growth under one roof. We build end-to-end.

Shared infrastructure

Legal, finance, ops, and tech stack shared across ventures. Reduces per-venture costs 40%. Faster launches.

Compounding learnings

What we learn in healthcare informs gaming. What we build for one venture becomes a tool for the next.

Evidence-based decisions

We kill ideas fast when they don't work. No sunk cost fallacy, no ego—just data.

Team philosophy

Small, senior teams

We hire senior operators with 5+ years shipping products. 10 people across 4 ventures. Each person owns a full problem—not just their piece of it.

Generalists over specialists

We value people who can think across disciplines. A designer who understands growth. An engineer who can talk to users.

Ownership, not execution

You won't just build what someone else scoped. You'll shape the problem, design the solution, and own the outcome.

Low ego, high standards

We're not interested in credentials or titles. We care about craft, curiosity, and whether you make the work better.
