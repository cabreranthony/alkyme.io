# Alkyme Website Content Audit - Current State

**Date**: 2026-05-03
**Auditor**: Claude (Automated Content Analysis)
**Business Model Reference**: ALKYME-BUSINESS-MODEL.md

---

## Executive Summary

The current Alkyme website effectively communicates the **Venture Studio** (Pillar 1) and **Bespoke AI/Tech Solutions** (Pillar 3) business models, but **completely omits Pillar 2: Marketing & Digital Support for SMBs**. The site is heavily AI-focused, which misrepresents the full scope of services and may alienate SMB clients seeking traditional marketing/digital services.

### Critical Findings:
- **Missing Business Pillar**: No mention of SMB marketing/digital services anywhere on the site
- **Over-indexing on AI**: 90%+ of messaging is AI-centric, creating false impression that Alkyme only does AI
- **Navigation Gap**: Current nav structure doesn't reflect all three business lines
- **Conversion Path Missing**: No clear path for SMB clients seeking non-AI services (web dev, branding, marketing)

---

## Current Navigation Structure

```
About | Labs | Solutions | Careers | Contact
```

### Issues:
1. "Labs" might be unclear to external audiences (could be "Ventures" or "Portfolio")
2. "Solutions" only captures Pillar 3 (tech licensing), not Pillar 2 (SMB services)
3. No dedicated entry point for traditional marketing/digital services
4. Footer mentions services not found in main nav (Supply Chain, E-Commerce, etc.)

## Navigation Structure Issues

### Current Structure
```
About | Labs | Solutions | Careers | Contact
```

### Problems:
1. "Labs" is insider jargon - could be "Ventures" or "Portfolio"
2. "Solutions" only represents Pillar 3
3. No entry point for Pillar 2 (SMB services)
4. Footer has links to industries not in main nav

### Recommended Structure - Option A
```
About | Ventures | Services | Solutions | Careers | Contact
```

**Rationale**:
- "Ventures" = Pillar 1 (portfolio companies)
- "Services" = Pillar 2 (SMB marketing/digital)
- "Solutions" = Pillar 3 (tech licensing)
- Each pillar gets equal weight

### Recommended Structure - Option B
```
About | What We Do ▼ | Portfolio | Careers | Contact
         ├─ Venture Studio
         ├─ Marketing Services
         └─ Technology Solutions
```

**Rationale**:
- Consolidates offerings under "What We Do"
- "Portfolio" shows all ventures
- Cleaner nav, fewer top-level items

### Recommended Structure - Option C
```
About | For Founders | For Businesses | Portfolio | Careers | Contact
```

**Rationale**:
- Audience-focused navigation
- "For Founders" = venture studio (Pillar 1)
- "For Businesses" = services + solutions (Pillar 2 + 3)
- Most user-centric approach

## Conclusion

The Alkyme website effectively communicates its venture studio capabilities (Pillar 1) and technology licensing model (Pillar 3), but **completely omits the SMB marketing & digital services business line (Pillar 2)**. This creates three major issues:

1. **Lost Revenue**: SMB clients seeking traditional services have no entry point and are actively turned away by the contact form FAQ.

2. **False Positioning**: The site creates the impression that Alkyme only does AI work, when the business model includes traditional marketing and digital services.

3. **Over-Reliance on AI Messaging**: 90%+ of content is AI-focused, which alienates clients who need non-AI solutions (web development, branding, operations automation, etc.).

### Highest Impact Changes:
1. **Add Services page** showcasing SMB marketing/digital offerings
2. **Update contact form** to include service inquiry option
3. **De-emphasize "AI"** in headlines and positioning
4. **Add navigation link** to Services
5. **Show non-AI solutions** from ventures (Epoch² tools, ops platforms, etc.)

By implementing these changes, the website will accurately represent all three business pillars and create conversion paths for SMB clients, mid-market companies seeking proven tech, and venture partners/investors.
