## SECTION: WHAT IT'S LIKE

H2
What you'll actually do

Body
Alkyme is a startup studio based in Los Angeles. We originate ventures, build them in-house, and spin them out when they're ready to scale. You might join the studio team or work directly on a growing venture.

Cards:

Own real outcomes
You're not executing someone else's roadmap. You're shaping product, talking to users, and making calls that affect what ships.

Work across the full loop
Product, growth, and strategy stay connected. You see how your work translates into user behavior.

Build things that last
Ventures are designed to become standalone companies. The impact you ship here compounds beyond a single sprint.

## SECTION: HOW WE WORK

H2
The environment

Grid:

Fast feedback loops
We test ideas with users early and often. You'll know quickly if something resonates.

Clarity over consensus
We make decisions based on signal, not group agreement. Direct feedback is part of the process.

Room to grow
You'll work on problems you haven't solved before. We expect you to figure some of it out as you go.

Small, focused teams
No layers of management. You'll work directly with the people building and launching the product.

Craft that compounds
The systems we build improve how we operate. Your work makes the next venture easier to launch.

Bias to action
We move forward with incomplete information. Waiting for perfect clarity isn't an option.
