## SECTION: HOW WE OPERATE (`#mission-heading`)

**Region label**
How we operate

**Lede**
We don't run programs for external founders or take on project work. Alkyme builds its own ventures. Strategy, product, and go-to-market stay in one conversation so market feedback drives what we build next.

**Split feature — H2**
From idea to independent company

**Body (glass)**
Most studios operate like service businesses or passive investors. We originate the ideas, prototype in days, validate with real users, and scale what works. The ventures that gain traction eventually spin out as independent companies with their own teams and cap tables.

Every venture teaches us something. Those lessons inform our systems, our playbooks, and the next bet. We focus on themes where software, operations, and distribution compound: infrastructure, workflows, and commerce.

## NOTES FOR EDITORS

- **Voice alignment**: This page uses the confident, direct, grounded voice from **`brand-content-guidelines.md`**. Avoid "we believe" or aspirational language—show outcomes instead.
- **Studio vs venture clarity**: We originate our own ventures (not an accelerator for external founders). Keep this distinction clear in all copy.
- **External links**: Breezy jobs site uses `rel="noopener noreferrer"` for security.
- **Accessibility**: Carousel slide images use empty `alt` where adjacent copy provides context; pillar and feature images retain descriptive alts. The name-band legacy block is a native `<details>` / `<summary>` disclosure (keyboard-expandable; do not strip focus styles in CSS).
- **Localization**: **`es/about.html`** and **`tl/about.html`** mirror this structure; update accordion panel copy and labels there when you change EN (ES/TL body columns may still be EN until translated).
