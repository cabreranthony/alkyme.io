## SECTION: HOW WE THINK ABOUT IT

H2
AI as infrastructure

Body
We treat AI the same way we treat databases or version control. It's part of the stack. It doesn't make strategic calls or decide what to build. It handles repetitive work so the team can focus on the things that matter: talking to users, making product decisions, and finding what resonates.

---

## SECTION: AN EXAMPLE

H2
Validating a new idea

Body
When we started exploring a new venture concept, we used AI to scan positioning across dozens of competitor sites. It surfaced gaps we hadn't considered. We tested messaging around those gaps with early users and adjusted based on what resonated.

A pass that would have taken days manually took an afternoon. The strategic decision about which direction to pursue still came from the team.

## SECTION: QUESTIONS

H2
Common questions

Q: Do you use AI to make decisions?
A: No. AI generates options. We decide which ones to test and what to ship based on user feedback.

Q: How do you validate what AI produces?
A: Everything gets tested. Prototypes go in front of users. Copy gets A/B tested. Features ship based on performance, not because a model suggested them.

Q: What about user data?
A: We don't train models on customer data. Internal processes only.

---

## SECTION: THE BOTTOM LINE

Body
AI helps us test more ideas in less time. It doesn't replace the work of understanding users, shaping product direction, or deciding what's worth building. Those decisions still take judgment, and judgment still comes from people.
