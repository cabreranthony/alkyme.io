# Alkymē Business Model - Three Core Areas

**Date**: 2026-05-03
**Purpose**: Define Alkymē's three business lines to guide site structure, navigation, and content strategy

---

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Venture Studio
**"Build companies and send them into the wild"**

- Build, incubate, and launch new companies
- Portfolio company development
- Take companies from 0→1→scale
- Eventually spin out into independent ventures
- **Example**: Healthcare AI companies, logistics platforms, etc.

**Site Implications:**
- Should have its own section (possibly Labs or Ventures)
- Showcase portfolio companies
- Highlight success stories and exits
- Attract potential founders/partners

---

### 2. Marketing & Digital Support for SMBs
**"Marketing and digital support for small businesses"**

- Full-service marketing and digital transformation
- Website development, digital strategy, branding
- Support for small businesses who need digital presence
- Traditional agency-style services

**Site Implications:**
- Needs clear service offering section
- Case studies from small business clients
- Service packages and pricing (potentially)
- Contact flow for service inquiries

---

### 3. Bespoke AI/Tech Solutions
**"Using learnings and AI from ventures to build solutions for SMB/midmarket clients"**

- License technology developed for portfolio companies
- Custom AI implementations for enterprise/midmarket
- Battle-tested platforms adapted to client needs
- Technology transfer from ventures to commercial clients
- **Current page**: Solutions page (but needs broader scope)

**Site Implications:**
- Current "Solutions" page focuses too heavily on AI
- Should emphasize "proven technology from real companies"
- Not all clients need AI - some need ops automation, CRM, logistics, etc.
- Leverage learnings from building actual companies

---

## Current Site Structure Issues

### Navigation
Current nav: About | Labs | Solutions | Careers | Contact

**Problems:**
1. "Solutions" only captures pillar #3, not pillars #1 or #2
2. "Labs" might be confusing - could be Ventures/Portfolio
3. No clear entry point for SMB marketing services (#2)
4. Too AI-heavy throughout (not every client needs AI)

### Suggested Navigation Structure

**Option A - Three Equal Pillars:**
- About
- Ventures (portfolio companies)
- Services (marketing/digital for SMBs)
- Solutions (tech/AI licensing)
- Careers
- Contact

**Option B - Consolidated:**
- About
- What We Do (dropdown)
  - Ventures
  - Services
  - Solutions
- Portfolio
- Careers
- Contact

**Option C - Client-Focused:**
- About
- For Founders (venture studio)
- For Businesses (services + solutions combined)
- Portfolio
- Careers
- Contact

## Content Strategy

### Messaging Priorities:
1. **Not just AI** - Alkymē builds complete businesses, not just AI wrappers
2. **Battle-tested** - Real companies, real users, real traction
3. **Three ways to work with us** - Venture partnership, services, or licensing
4. **Learnings → Solutions** - What we build in ventures becomes client solutions

### Tone Shifts Needed:
- Less "AI-first" language
- More "we build companies that work"
- Emphasize operational excellence, not just technology
- Show the full spectrum: branding, ops, tech, growth

---

## Next Steps

1. **Site Architecture Redesign**
   - Restructure navigation to reflect three pillars
   - Create clear pathways for different customer types
   - Separate venture portfolio from commercial services

2. **Content Audit**
   - Remove over-emphasis on AI
   - Add marketing/digital service offerings
   - Expand portfolio/venture showcase
   - Balance technical and business value props

3. **Solutions Page Rewrite**
   - Current content is too narrow (AI-only)
   - Should show full spectrum of offerings from ventures
   - Include non-AI solutions (logistics, ops, CRM, etc.)
   - De-emphasize "AI" in headlines, emphasize "proven solutions"

4. **New Pages to Consider**
   - Services page (marketing/digital for SMBs)
   - Portfolio/Ventures page (showcase companies)
   - Potentially separate Solutions into categories

## Key Insight
**Alkymē's differentiator**: We don't just build technology - we build real companies with real customers. Then we take what works and make it available to other businesses. This is fundamentally different from:
- Traditional agencies (we've built companies, not just websites)
- AI consultancies (our AI is battle-tested in production)
- SaaS platforms (we customize to your exact workflow)

**Our superpower**: The knowledge and tech stack from building successful ventures
