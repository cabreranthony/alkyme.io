Common questions

Do you take investment in your ventures?

Yes. When a venture shows traction, we spin it out as a standalone company and bring in external capital. We're selective about partners and typically work with strategic investors who bring more than money.

Can you build something custom for my company?

We build ventures, not client projects. But you can license the platforms we've already built and proven. Contact us to discuss which platform fits your needs.

How do I apply for a role?

Use the form above and select "Joining the team." Include links to things you've shipped and a brief note about what you're looking to do. We review all applications within a week.

Do you mentor or advise other founders?

Occasionally, but we're selective. If you're building something in our domain (AI systems for overlooked industries, entertainment tech), we're more likely to engage. Send us a note.
