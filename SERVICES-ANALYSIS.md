## Recommendations for New Site

### 1. Three-Pillar Homepage Structure

**Section 1: Ventures**
- "We build companies from the ground up"
- Showcase 3-4 portfolio companies
- CTA: "Explore Portfolio" or "Partner With Us"

**Section 2: Services**
- "Full-service marketing and digital for growing businesses"
- Feature top 3-4 service packages
- CTA: "View Services" or "Start a Project"

**Section 3: Solutions**
- "Battle-tested technology from real companies"
- Feature 2-3 key solution areas (Healthcare AI, Operations, etc.)
- CTA: "Explore Solutions" or "Book Consultation"

### 2. Services Page Restructure

**Primary Services (Packages):**
- Digital Foundation
- Growth Marketing
- Brand & Creative
- Business Operations

**Each package should include:**
- Clear service list
- Example deliverables
- Typical timeline
- Case study or testimonial
- Pricing indicator or "Contact for quote"

### 3. Solutions Page Enhancement

**Don't call it "AI Solutions" - call it "Technology Solutions" or "Proven Platforms"**

**Structure:**
- Industry-specific solutions (Healthcare, Logistics, Retail)
- Functional solutions (Customer Intelligence, Operations, Analytics)
- Custom development capabilities
- Emphasize "used by real companies with real customers"

### 4. Portfolio/Ventures Page

**Content:**
- Active portfolio companies
- Exit stories (if any)
- Venture building process
- How to partner with Alkymē
- Founder testimonials

## Final Recommendation

**The new Alkymē website should:**

1. **Lead with the three-pillar model** on the homepage
2. **Feature SMB services prominently** (this appears to be current main revenue stream)
3. **Showcase ventures/portfolio** to establish credibility and differentiation
4. **Position tech solutions** as the premium offering that leverages venture learnings
5. **Connect the dots** between building companies → learning what works → offering proven solutions

**The current alkyme.io services page is valuable** but doesn't tell the full story. It positions Alkymē as a capable agency, but misses the unique venture studio + technology licensing model that sets the company apart.

**Key insight**: Most agencies can offer marketing services. Only Alkymē can say "we've used these exact strategies to build successful companies, and now we're bringing that expertise to your business."
