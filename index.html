Our Process

From idea to market

01

Validate Fast

Most ideas fail because nobody wants them. We talk to real customers before writing a single line of code. If we can't find 10 people willing to pay, we kill it and move on.

  • Customer conversations in week 1, not month 6
  • Rapid prototypes to test demand, not demonstrate "vision"
  • Kill bad ideas in days, not after burning $50K
  • Only build what customers say they'll actually pay for
02

Ship in Weeks

No 6-month roadmaps. No "phases." We get you to market fast using platforms we've already built. First version ships in 4-8 weeks. Real customers, real feedback, real iteration.

  • Launch in 4-8 weeks using proven tech stacks
  • Get paying customers before perfect features
  • Daily standups with your team, not monthly check-ins
  • Reuse what's already working across our ventures
03

Scale or Spin Out

Hit product-market fit? You become a standalone company with your core team, dedicated funding, and continued guidance from people who've scaled businesses before. Still flatlining? We shut it down fast and move to the next opportunity.

  • Spin out when revenue proves the model works
  • Your team gets equity and runs the show
  • We stay involved as advisors, not micromanagers
  • Kill what's not working—fast and without drama
