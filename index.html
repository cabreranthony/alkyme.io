Our Process

From idea to market

01

Identify

We identify high-potential opportunities through market analysis, domain expertise, and systematic validation of customer needs.

  • Analyze market gaps and emerging opportunities
  • Conduct deep customer discovery and validation
  • Leverage domain expertise from previous ventures
  • Build rapid prototypes to test core hypotheses
02

Build

Shared teams across engineering, product, and design build systematically. Early customer feedback drives rapid iteration and refinement.

  • Dedicated cross-functional teams for each venture
  • Continuous customer engagement and user testing
  • Comprehensive data instrumentation and analytics
  • Shared infrastructure and technical resources
03

Scale

Proven ventures transition to standalone companies with dedicated teams, strategic guidance, and continued operational support from the studio.

  • Transition when key metrics demonstrate product-market fit
  • Core team continues with equity and leadership roles
  • Strategic capital partnerships or path to profitability
  • Ongoing strategic guidance and board participation
