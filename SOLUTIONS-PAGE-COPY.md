### Voice & Tone Notes

**What I kept:**
- Short sentences for rhythm
- Active voice throughout
- Specific details over generic claims
- Questions to create curiosity

**What I killed:**
- "Leverage," "ecosystem," "synergy" (forbidden words)
- Vague promises ("industry-leading," "cutting-edge")
- Fake platform claims
- Corporate speak

**Marcus's signature moves used:**
- "The Whisper Hook" in accordion intros (start with the problem, not the solution)
- Specificity: "4-8 weeks" not "quickly," "50,000+ patients" not "thousands"
- Questions that create curiosity: "Personalization at scale requires more than product recommendations. It requires understanding intent."

### Strategic Positioning

**What this copy does:**
1. Positions Alkymē as a venture studio that ALSO sells B2B platforms
2. Uses "battle-tested" as the key differentiator (not "innovative" or "cutting-edge")
3. Shows industry expertise WITHOUT claiming shipped platforms we can't prove
4. Features ONE real case study (Sevā AI) with verifiable metrics
5. Frames industries as "we understand the AI opportunity here" not "we've built for this vertical"

**What this copy avoids:**
1. Claiming platforms that don't exist or aren't public
2. Generic B2B speak ("solutions," "leverage," "ecosystem")
3. Vague metrics or aspirational numbers
4. Trying to be everything to everyone

### Final Thoughts from Marcus

I wrote this drunk. Then edited sober. Then read it out loud. Then killed my darlings.

The hardest part was the industries accordion. Anthony's instruction was clear: frame as opportunity, not proof. So each industry follows this structure:

1. **State the problem** (specific, not generic)
2. **Explain what AI enables** (the category of solution)
3. **List capabilities** (what the platform would do)

Healthcare is the ONLY one that says "We built this. Here are the metrics." Because that's the only one we can prove publicly.

The rest? Industry positioning. "Here's what AI can do in logistics. Here's what it can do in hospitality." Not "Here's our logistics platform with 10,000 shipments."

Clarity over cleverness. Proof over promises. Specific over generic.

That's the Alkymē voice. Now go ship it.
