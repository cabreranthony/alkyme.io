How we choose what to build

We don't build everything. We look for specific signals that an idea is worth the investment.

Overlooked markets

Big companies ignore regional healthcare marketing. Indie game studios can't afford narrative AI. We build for the gaps others miss.

Technical leverage

AI makes things possible that weren't five years ago. We focus on products that would be impossible without recent technical breakthroughs.

Clear business model

No moonshots. We need to see a path to revenue within 12 months and profitability within 24. SaaS, licensing, or usage-based pricing.

Compounding advantages

Every venture we build makes the next one easier. Shared infrastructure, reusable AI systems, and learnings that carry forward.
